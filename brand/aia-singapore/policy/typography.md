# Typography

Sources: AIA Brand Standards v2.0 pp. 73–77 · design.aia.com/typography

## Typefaces
- **AIA Everest** (bespoke): primary for everything, print and digital. It is "bold, welcoming, positive and confident."
  Styles: Regular, Regular Italic, Medium, Medium Italic, Bold, **Extra Bold**, **Condensed**, **Condensed Medium**.
  OTF and web fonts are on the Group Brand asset-links page (p140). **A signed font release is required before sharing with partners.**
- **Arial**: the system fallback, only when AIA Everest isn't possible.
- **Open Sans**: digital (app and web) body text alongside Everest.
- **Chinese: Monotype Hei** (MHeiPRC for Simplified, MHeiHK for Traditional). Bilingual pairings by stroke weight:
  | Chinese | English |
  |---|---|
  | MHei Medium | AIA Everest Regular |
  | MHei Bold | AIA Everest Medium |
  | MHei Xbold | AIA Everest Bold |
  It needs a commercial licence. Digital fallback: **Noto Sans SC/TC**.
- **Tamil and Malay**: no brand guidance. Use Noto Sans Tamil (per the Qi localisation rule) and Everest or Open Sans for Malay. **Gap: confirm with AIA SG.**

> The Studio needs the Everest OTF files plus a font release, and ideally an MHei licence. Until then it renders with Arial or Open Sans for Latin and Noto Sans SC for Chinese, and flags `font_substituted: true`.

## Hierarchy (p76). Hard ratios.
- **Sub-heading = 1/3 of the headline point size.**
- **Body = 1/2 of the sub-heading size.**
- **Minimum body size: 10 pt print, 15 px digital.**
- Headlines in **AIA Red** (on light) or **white on AIA Red**. Sub-headings in AIA Charcoal (white on red; see the contrast note in `colour.md`). Body in AIA Red or Charcoal, white on red.
- Set the headline and the most important content in AIA Red so it stands out.
- Observed style across brand examples: **headlines in ALL CAPS, AIA Everest Bold or Condensed**, often stacked over 2–4 short lines ("DO IT / FOR / YOU", "LET'S DO IT WELL").

## Writing systems (design.aia.com)
- Alphabetic (English, Malay): standard line height.
- Tall (**Tamil**, Thai, Hindi, Vietnamese, Sinhala): extra line height.
- Dense (**Chinese**, Korean): extra line height.
Proposed multipliers are in `tokens/typography.json`.
