# Design Principles

Sources: AIA Brand Standards v2.0 p48 (marketing) · design.aia.com/design-standards (digital)

## Brand design principles (Brand Standards p48). Governs marketing collateral.
Our designs:
- are **dynamic and full of life**
- **inspire** customers to live Healthier, Longer, Better Lives
- are **easy to understand** (like a mentor)

Our artwork:
- **embraces AIA Red as the lead brand colour and uses it with confidence**
- is sensitive to the brief, whether bold or understated

> "Whatever we do, we are always, unmistakably AIA."

The assets (logos, colours, photography, Moving Mountains and so on) can be mixed and matched creatively (p49). This supports the BRD's "variations, not duplicates" principle: each creative direction can combine the assets differently.

## Digital design principles (AIA Qi)
| Principle | AIA checklist (summary) | Poster review criterion |
|---|---|---|
| **Clarity** | Clear next step? Simple language? Does the layout guide the eye? | One focal point, one clear CTA, scannable in about 3 s |
| **Efficiency** | No unnecessary steps | Minimal copy; key facts grouped |
| **Unified** | Consistent components and patterns | Only brand-pack tokens and assets |
| **Delightful** | Surprise and delight? | Each direction has a distinct idea |
| **Human** | Accessible and inclusive | Contrast ≥ 4.5:1; inclusive imagery; minimum sizes |
| **Purposeful** | Instils confidence in AIA? | Customer-benefit led, not salesy |

See `brand-checklist.md` for how the Brand Standards checklist maps to validators.
