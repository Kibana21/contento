# Moving Mountains: the key brand graphic

Source: AIA Brand Standards v2.0 pp. 57–71 (HNW variant p129–131, Vitality 3D variant p124)

The Moving Mountains are 3 or 4 overlapping rounded mountain shapes. They represent each customer's personal goals, dreams and journeys towards Healthier, Longer, Better Lives.
**They are the most distinctive AIA element after the logo.** A poster with correct Moving Mountains, a cut-out agent photo and a red headline reads instantly as AIA.

**For the Studio this is a deterministic renderer component, not an AI-generated image.**
- Source shapes: the official library on the Group Brand SharePoint (p70), or built from the Illustrator grid template (p71).
- Rendered as SVG with the tint and opacity rules below.
- **Never generated by an image model.** AI tends to produce "pointed triangles", which is an explicit don't.

## Construction (p71)
- Shapes are drawn on the official grid (reference 560 × 400 px). Each is a four-sided shape starting from a baseline grid point.
- **Minimum 3, maximum 4 mountains** in static form.
- Together the shapes must cover the full grid width. They need not fill its height.
- **8 px rounded corners** on every mountain (at grid scale).
- Back mountains are trimmed with "minus front" so there are no overlapping excess shapes.

## Colour variants
Mountains are numbered front to back. The first (front) mountain is the largest and lowest.

### Core, on white or light backgrounds (p59)
- **First mountain: always 100% AIA Red, 100% opacity.**
- First and last mountains are 100% opacity. **Middle mountains are 90% opacity.**
- 3 mountains: Red 100% (100% opacity) · Red 60% (90%) · Red 20% (100%)
- 4 mountains: Red 100% (100%) · Red 80% (90%) · Red 60% (90%) · Red 20% (100%)

### Inverted, only on AIA Red backgrounds (p59)
- 3 mountains: Red 60% (100%) · Red 40% (90%) · Red 20% (100%)
- 4 mountains: Red 60% (100%) · Red 40% (90%) · Red 20% (90%) · White (100%)

### Multicolour (p60)
- AIA Red must be predominant, either as the first mountain or as the background.
- Secondary colours are used **at 100% only. No tints.**
- On an AIA Red background, the first mountain may be a secondary colour.

### Two-colour (p61)
- AIA Red plus tints of **one** secondary colour. The first mountain is AIA Red.
- On a red background the first mountain is 100% of the chosen secondary colour. Don't mix tints of different colours.

### Outline (p62)
- 100% AIA Red or white stroke only, or the core tint rules. Choose the stroke thickness to suit. The inverted outline is white only. No secondary colours.

### Mirror (p63): **out of scope**
This is a designer-only InDesign technique (reflective fill with bevel) for AIA One Billion.

## Combining with photography (pp. 64–68). Maps to Studio layouts.
| Mode | How | Studio use |
|---|---|---|
| **Container** | The first mountain is an image mask. The second mountain is 100% AIA Red. Secondary colours are allowed on the 3rd and 4th. | Venue or lifestyle image inside a mountain |
| **Cutout** | A cutout photo sits in front of the mountains, or is cropped to the first mountain. Works with all variants. | **Agent portrait (background removed) in front of the mountains. The default "Human" direction.** |
| **Full bleed** | Full-bleed photo with the mountains as a supporting graphic | Premium Editorial with a lifestyle or venue image |
| **Layers** | Cutouts layered *inside* the mountains | Multi-speaker seminar (speakers inside different peaks) |
| **Transparent** | First mountain 100% Red. The others use core tints with a **multiply** blend. **Light images only.** | Festive image showing through the mountains |

## Don'ts (p69) → validator rules
1. Don't complicate the design with other graphic elements.
2. **No pointed triangles.** Use library assets or grid-built shapes (rounded corners).
3. Don't combine *container* with *cutout* photography.
4. **Don't rotate** the mountains. **Don't use them upside down.**
5. **Max 4 mountains.**
6. Inverted mountains only on an AIA Red background.
7. **Only one Moving Mountains graphic per design.**
8. Don't overuse them. They needn't appear on every page (for single posters, one is fine).
9. On secondary-colour backgrounds, only core (red) mountains.

## Variants reserved for other brands (do NOT use for agent collateral)
- **AIA Vitality 3D glass mountains**: only for Live With Vitality campaigns.
- **HNW Olive mountains**: only for AIA High Net Worth.
