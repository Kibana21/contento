# Tone of Voice and Writing

Sources: AIA Brand Standards v2.0 pp. 16–21 (primary) · design.aia.com /tone-of-voice, /writing-digital-content, /inclusivity
See also `brand-foundation.md` for the mentor persona and the Singapore cultural cluster.

## Five tone principles (Brand Standards p18)
| Principle | It means | Copy check |
|---|---|---|
| **Use simple language** | Short sentences, simple words, no acronyms, no jargon | Flesch ≥ 60; flag acronyms and jargon terms |
| **Speak to the individual** | Say "you", not "customers"; use names and titles; respect cultural differences | Second-person check; no "customers/clients are advised…" |
| **Strengthen with proof** | Show examples, back up with insight and facts; avoid generalisation and vagueness | Any claim needs a source (**for product/financial claims, see compliance**) |
| **Focus on the future** | Talk about the future, not the past; explain *why*, not just *what* | Future or benefit framing |
| **Be positive and inclusive** | Bold statements; part of something bigger; exclude no one | No fear-selling, no negative comparisons with others |

Examples from p19:
- ✅ "The healthier you start, the stronger you go on."
- ✅ "Living longer means living better with AIA – whatever that means for you."
- ❌ "Will you have enough savings to fund a long retirement?" (fear-based)
- ❌ "Customers are advised as a matter of standard protocol to seek independent financial advice…" (impersonal, bureaucratic)

## Tone spectrum (p20–21)
From more corporate to more conversational:

| Channel | Mentor sounds | Studio campaign families |
|---|---|---|
| Product documentation | assuring, calm, professional | (product disclaimers and legal text) |
| Corporate comms | professional, confident, inspiring | Seminar, Career/Recruitment, Achievement |
| **Marketing comms** (ads, product info, **social/blogs**) | **inspiring, compassionate, emotional** | Festive, Relationship, Personal Branding, Educational |
| Customer interactions | emotional, conversational, familiar | Birthday, Thank-you, Client appreciation |

Example social copy (p21): *"This chunky Vietnamese pickle with coriander seeds recipe is a fun, tasty way to a healthier diet."* This is positive and infectiously enthusiastic.

## Voice keywords (design.aia.com)
Confident · Human · Positive · Inclusive. These are consistent with the persona above.

## Readability (design.aia.com)
- Aim for a lower-secondary reading level. Use short, simple sentences.
- Proposed poster target: Flesch Reading Ease ≥ 60. Warn below 60, fail below 50.
- Flesch bands: 90–100 very easy · 80–90 easy · 70–80 fairly easy · 60–70 plain English · 50–60 fairly difficult · 30–50 difficult · 0–30 very difficult.

## Inclusivity (design.aia.com)
- Use gender-neutral language; no binary "he/she" for individuals.
- Use people-first language; never label people by disability or condition.
- Write accurate alt text. → Exported social assets include suggested alt text.
- Use clear, precise language that works with assistive technology.

## Mechanical rules (validator)
- "Purpose" is capitalised when it means AIA's Purpose. "Healthier, Longer, Better Lives" is title case.
- Avoid acronyms. If one is unavoidable, expand it on first use.
