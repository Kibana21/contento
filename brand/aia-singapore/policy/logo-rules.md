# Logo Rules

Source: AIA Brand Standards v2.0 pp. 31–40, 110–112

**Status:** rules are documented. **Logo files are still needed.** They sit on the Group Brand SharePoint (asset-links page, p140, internal use only).
Hard rule (BRD ADR-002): logos are placed only as sourced vector assets and are never generated or redrawn by AI.

## The three logos
| Logo | What it is | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **HLBL Logo Lockup** | Corporate Logo + "Healthier, Longer, Better Lives" (horizontal) | **Preferred. Use first, in almost all cases.** |
| **Corporate Logo** | Himalayas mountain symbol + AIA | Not enough space for HLBL; app icons; **legal, regulatory and T&Cs documents** |
| **Wordmark** | "AIA" letters only | Only (1) legibility at distance or small size (signage, pens) or (2) sub-brands. Rare. |

**Studio default:** HLBL Logo Lockup (English). Use the Corporate Logo only for disclaimers-heavy or legal layouts. Never use the Wordmark for agent collateral.

## Clear space and minimum size
Y = height of the Corporate Logo symbol.

| Logo | Clear space (all sides) | Min print | Min digital |
|---|---|---|---|
| HLBL Logo Lockup | 0.25 Y | 10 mm | 24 px |
| Corporate Logo | 0.25 Y | 10 mm | 24 px |
| Wordmark | 0.35 × height of "A" | 3 mm | 15 px |
| AIA One Billion (standalone) | 0.5 X | 5 mm | 20 px |
| AIA One Billion lockup | 0.5 X | 10 mm | 60 px |
| AIA Vitality | 0.5 X | 10 mm | 24 px |

- **Clear space is also the minimum distance to the page edge.**
- The logo may be resized freely above the minimum.
- Recommended poster sizing (proposed): logo height of about 6–8% of the canvas short edge (about 64–88 px at 1080) for social.

## Placement (p38)
- Allowed positions: **top left / centre / right, centred in the middle, bottom left / centre / right.**
- It must not get lost in the information hierarchy.
- Stationery, legal and regulatory documents: Corporate Logo **top left only**.
→ Template families pick from these 7 anchor positions only.

## Colour
- **Logos are only ever AIA Red (`#D31145`) or white.** No other colours.
- White logo on AIA Red or on dark or busy imagery; red logo on white or light backgrounds.

## Don'ts (pp. 39–40) → validator checks
1. Don't alter, redesign or redraw any element. Don't deconstruct it to create other identities.
2. Don't recolour it (only red or white).
3. Don't place it on a background without sufficient contrast. Don't add an outline; move the logo or change the image instead.
4. Don't alter the HLBL lockup's internal relationships.
5. **Don't place text so close to the Corporate Logo that it can be confused with the HLBL lockup.**
   → Agent name, agency name and campaign titles must stay outside the clear space and be clearly separated from the logo.
6. Wordmark: don't lock it up with the Purpose. Don't use it where the Corporate Logo or HLBL should be used.

## Localised versions (p33)
- Localised HLBL lockups exist for China, Hong Kong and Taiwan (Chinese).
- **Use the English version wherever possible.** New local versions need Group Brand Team and Group CMO approval.
→ AIA Singapore uses the **English HLBL lockup**, including on bilingual (English–Chinese) posters.

## Co-branding (p45)
Sponsorships and partnerships use only the Corporate Logo or HLBL lockup, never the Wordmark. They need Group Brand Team approval.
Lockup rules: partner logo at equal visual prominence; an AIA Charcoal keyline divider as tall as the AIA logo; gaps of 0.25 × the AIA logo height.
→ Out of scope for Phase 1. Agents may not create co-branded lockups.
