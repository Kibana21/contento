# Imagery: Photography, Illustration, Iconography, Infographics

Sources: AIA Brand Standards v2.0 pp. 78–94 · design.aia.com /photography, /illustration, /iconography

## Photography
"We are not interested in posing models or unobtainable lifestyles." Photography is inclusive, vibrant and full of life. It shows healthy lifestyles, friends, family, mental wellness and a passion for living.

**Photography should:**
- represent Healthier, Longer, Better Lives
- show real life and real people, captured in the moment
- be inclusive and realistic, showing a variety of life circumstances
- show emotions that feel up close and personal

**Brief essentials (p80). Use these as the image-generation prompt scaffold:**
- **Customer-centric**: people are the focus, not landscapes or objects. Authentic, intimate, full of infectious energy.
- **Active**: never static or posed. Vibrancy and living in the moment (not only sport).
- **Personal**: relatable and inspiring. Viewers should recognise themselves.
- **Progress**: suggest the joy of making progress, however small.

**Don'ts (p82) → image-generation negative constraints and reviewer checks:**
1. **No landscape photography** or images where people aren't the main focus.
   → A festive *background* image may be scenic, but the composition's focal point must be a person (the agent cutout or lifestyle people).
2. No overly posed images.
3. No negative imagery.
4. **No single-use plastics or environmentally damaging materials** (ESG policy). Show reusable bottles and containers.
5. No unrealistic body images.
Also (design.aia.com): no stereotypes. Show diverse ethnicities, abilities, ages and identities; use warm lighting.

**The agent portrait is exempt from generation.** It is the agent's real photo: only crop, cut out, relight or colour-balance it (BRD §18).
Agent photos should still follow the spirit: natural, warm and approachable rather than stiff studio poses. The Studio can give guidance when the agent uploads photos.

## Illustration (p84–88)
- Style: **"Everyday surrealism"**. Dreams and ambitions are central; it plays with scale and wit, subverts everyday objects, uses subtle facial features, and has distinctive shading and texture.
- **AIA Red must always be present**, with secondary colours for other elements.
- Multi-illustration scenes use grey backgrounds. The Moving Mountains may be a backdrop (cropped landscape or minimal).
- **Only use illustration where photography isn't appropriate. Never in above-the-line communications.**
- Allowed: web and app product info, explainer videos, internal comms, annual reports, infographics, **email marketing, social media posts**.
→ The Studio may use illustration for the *Educational* family and social posts. For festive and seminar posters, prefer photography. Generated illustrations must imitate the everyday-surrealism style and be flagged as generated.

## Iconography (p90–91)
- **System icons**: minimal, modern, rounded edges. Standalone or in a circular container. Secondary colours are allowed for navigation.
- **Illustrated icons** (non-UX: product info, notifications, highlights, infographics): simple shapes; **AIA Red must be present; 2–3 colours max**.
- Product-category icons (Health, Life, Accident, MPF) are for identifying products only. → Phase 2 (product campaigns) only.
- Only use icons from the AIA icon library. → Event-detail icons (date, time, location) must come from the library; library files are needed.

## Infographics (p93–94)
- They put information first and make complex ideas easy to understand.
- **Bold style** for reporting, presentations and product materials. **Minimal outline style** for digital and interactive use.
- AIA Red is always present. Secondary colours are allowed for legibility, with enough contrast between them.
→ Relevant to the *Educational* family (Phase 2).

## Motion and sound (p96–98). For future video.
- Moving Mountains as a metaphor for change (never static). **Upward movement** everywhere. Reactive to the user.
- Character animation: everyday surrealism with a positive "bounce".
- Music: positive, active, forward motion. Voiceover sounds like a mentor (the 8 traits).
