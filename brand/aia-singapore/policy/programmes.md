# Sub-brands and Programmes

Source: AIA Brand Standards v2.0 pp. 41–46, 108–135

These rules mostly restrict what agents may do. Phase 1 agent collateral uses the **master AIA brand only**.

## Sub-brands and endorsements (p42–43)
- Create a sub-brand only when necessary. Usually a name is enough, without a new identity.
- Sub-brands use the Wordmark plus AIA Everest, with "AIA" first. They need local brand team approval (Group approval for key businesses).
- Endorsement lines ("An AIA Company", "Powered by AIA") go **bottom left**. Text in AIA Everest Regular, AIA Charcoal; "AIA" in Everest Bold, AIA Red.
→ **Agency or team names are not sub-brands.** Render them as plain text in the agent contact block, never as a logo lockup. Whether agency-level marks are allowed is an open question for AIA SG.

## AIA One Billion (AOB) (p109–116)
- AIA's commitment to engage 1 billion people in Asia to live HLBL by 2030. Tagline: "join the journey".
- Logo: standalone or lockup. Clear space 0.5X. Minimum 5 mm / 20 px (standalone), 10 mm / 60 px (lockup).
- Localisation: only "join the journey" may be translated. "AIA One Billion" always stays in English.
- With HLBL: align heights and baselines; place in opposite corners.
- Video end screens are AIA Red with both logos.
→ Possible later: an optional "purpose-led" badge for community or wellness events. Out of Phase 1.

## AIA Vitality (p117–125)
- Wellness programme. "Live With Vitality" campaigns. The logo is the AIA Wordmark plus the Vitality logo. Clear space 0.5X, minimum 10 mm / 24 px.
- Designs always feature AIA Red. **3D glass Moving Mountains are only for Vitality.**
- Status colours: Silver → AIA Blue, Gold → AIA Red, Platinum → AIA Purple.
- Example poster copy pattern: "[VERB PHRASE] WITH VITALITY" + short body + QR + "Find out more".
→ Not in Phase 1. Needs the Live With Vitality toolkit.

## AIA High Net Worth (HNW) (p126–135)
- Premium sub-identity: **AIA Olive replaces AIA Red**, with Dark Green, Warm Grey, Charcoal, white and gold foil.
- Photography is premium and aspirational, with neutral tones. Wealth is shown *subtly* (style, clothing), never with stereotypes such as private jets.
→ **Do not use for general agents.** A "premium" creative direction must still use the master red palette. HNW styling needs explicit HNW entitlement.

## Sponsorship and partnerships (p45–46)
Corporate Logo or HLBL only. Partner logo at equal prominence. Charcoal keyline divider. Group Brand approval. The AIA Spurs partnership has separate guidelines (via CSM).
→ Out of scope for agents.
