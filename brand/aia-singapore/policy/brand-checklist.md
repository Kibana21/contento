# Brand Checklist → Studio Validators

Source: AIA Brand Standards v2.0 pp. 137–139. It was added to the AIA Corporate Policy Portal with Compliance and Risk, so **the standards are policy in all markets.**

## Positioning checklist (p138) → LLM copy and design reviewer rubric
| # | Question | Reviewer signal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does it clearly show our Purpose? Does it represent being there every day to support healthier living? | HLBL lockup present; message links to healthier, longer, better living |
| 2 | Does it look and sound **active**? Does it point to growth, progress, improvement? | Forward and progress framing |
| 3 | Is it **customer-centric**? Does it focus on the customer outcome? | "You"-focused, benefit-led |
| 4 | Is it **personal**, inclusive and accepting? | No stereotypes; individual framing |
| 5 | Does it embody the **mentor**? Does it remove un-mentor-like elements? | Score against the IS/ISN'T lists |
| 6 | Do the mentor traits come across in look *and* feel? | Warm, confident, not aggressive visuals |
| 7 | Does it use a **mentor tone of voice**, adapted to the audience? | 5 tone principles; tone spectrum by channel |
| 8 | Does it account for **cultural insights** in the market? | Singapore = Emancipation / "On your side" |

## Identity checklist (p139) → deterministic validators
| # | Check | Validator |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HLBL Logo Lockup used (or a documented reason not to)? | `logo.variant == hlbl` unless the layout flags legal/space |
| 2 | Wordmark only in its rare cases? | Wordmark forbidden for agent collateral |
| 3 | Core and secondary colours applied correctly? | red dominant; ≤ 4 secondary; secondary requires red; approved backgrounds; no gradients |
| 4 | Moving Mountains correct? | 3–4 mountains; one graphic; tint and opacity tables; no rotation or flip; inverted only on red; no container + cutout |
| 5 | Photography on brand? | multimodal reviewer: people-focused, candid, no plastics, no landscape-only |
| 6 | Right illustration style? | illustration not used in above-the-line; red present |
| 7 | Icons in the right style? | library icons only; illustrated icons have 2–3 colours including red |
| 8 | Infographics in the correct style? | (Phase 2) |
| 9–10 | Motion and sound principles | (future video) |

## Plus rules from the rest of the book
- Logo: red or white only; clear space 0.25Y; minimum 24 px; one of the 7 allowed positions; no text crowding the Corporate Logo.
- Typography: subhead = headline/3; body = subhead/2; body ≥ 15 px digital or 10 pt print.
- Copy: capitalise "Purpose" and "Healthier, Longer, Better Lives".
