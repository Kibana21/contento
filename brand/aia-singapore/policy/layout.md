# Grid, Layout and Spacing

Sources: design.aia.com /grid-and-layout, /spacing · AIA Brand Standards v2.0 (placement p38, examples pp. 54, 102, 104, 114, 125)

## Published
- **12-column grid** (digital). Rows have 1–12 equal columns, and columns combine.
- **4px soft grid**: all spacing is divisible by 4 and aligns to 8.
- Spacing principles: group related items; tighter spacing for dense information and looser for focused content; use tokens consistently.
- **Logo anchor positions** (Brand p38): top L/C/R, centre, bottom L/C/R. The logo's clear space doubles as its edge margin.

## Patterns observed in the Brand Standards' marketing examples
- **Social post** (p114): Moving Mountains take roughly the top half. A photo sits in a mountain container or as a cutout. The logo is in a corner.
- **Poster** (p114): a full-bleed lifestyle photo. A stacked ALL-CAPS red headline on the left ("DO IT / FOR / YOU"). Mountains on the right. Logos at bottom left and right.
- **Outdoor** (p54): a photo inside a mountain container, a white panel, a red ALL-CAPS headline, HLBL top left.
- **Vitality poster** (p125): an ALL-CAPS stacked headline, short body, a QR code at bottom right, and a "Find out more" CTA.
→ These become the seed **template families** (Human / Premium Editorial / Information First / Contemporary Social).

## Proposed poster adaptation (needs sign-off)
- A 12-column grid on every canvas. Outer margin is about 6% of the short edge, rounded to a multiple of 4 (1080 → 64 px). The margin must be ≥ the logo clear space.
- Snap every position and gap to 4 px (prefer 8).
- Spacing scale: `tokens/spacing.json`.
- Social safe zones are defined per output preset (for example Instagram Story top and bottom UI).
