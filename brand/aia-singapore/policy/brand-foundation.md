# Brand Foundation: Purpose, Persona, Culture

Source: AIA Brand Standards v2.0 (brands.pdf) pp. 4–29

## Purpose
**Healthier, Longer, Better Lives (HLBL).** It is:
- **Active**: always moving and making progress.
- **Customer-centric**: customers are at the heart of everything.
- **Personal**: it means something different to everyone ("For my family", "For my career", "For my future").

Ambition: *We help people live · We help people grow · We are always there.*

**Writing rules (hard):**
- Capitalise "Purpose" in "our Purpose" or "AIA's Purpose".
- Capitalise each word of "Healthier, Longer, Better Lives".
→ Copy validator: regex check for the lower-case forms.

## Brand persona: AIA as a mentor
A mentor is an experienced, trusted adviser who helps you find your own way. They are never harsh, critical or aggressive.

| A mentor IS | A mentor ISN'T |
|---|---|
| Compassionate | Harsh |
| Positive | Negative |
| Confident | Inexperienced |
| Straightforward | Confusing |
| Encouraging | Judgemental |
| Motivating | Passive |
| Guiding | In charge |
| On your side | Arrogant |

### The 8 personality traits
- **Compassionate**: empathetic, sees the customer's view, never judgemental.
- **Straightforward**: simple and to the point, direct and respectful, not long-winded.
- **Positive**: optimistic, focused on opportunity, energetic but grounded.
- **Confident**: proud of history and expertise, knowledgeable without arrogance.
- **Encouraging**: gently supportive, builds a "can do" mentality, never too directive.
- **Motivating**: inspiring; shows rather than tells and provides evidence.
- **Guiding**: indicates a way forward and gives options; instructive, not prescriptive.
- **On your side**: champions what matters to customers; inclusive and celebratory.

**Why this matters for the Studio:** the agent is literally a financial *adviser*. The mentor persona is a natural voice for agent collateral: warm and guiding, never pushy or salesy. The LLM copy reviewer should score drafts against the IS/ISN'T lists.

## Cultural guidance: Singapore
AIA groups its 18 markets into four cultural clusters. **Singapore is in the Emancipation cluster**, along with Australia, Hong Kong, Macau, New Zealand, South Korea and Taiwan.

| | Emancipation |
|---|---|
| HLBL is about | Self-confidence and rediscovery |
| People feel | Things need to change |
| They want | To feel achievement and validation |
| **Leading mentor trait** | **On your side**: champion the causes that matter, be inclusive, celebrate individuals |
| A mentor here | Slows down and reflects · finds new options · lets individuals define their own success · is simple, honest, authentic · helps reconnect with a simpler way of life |

→ Default copy guidance for AIA Singapore: celebrate the individual's own definition of success and use honest, unpretentious language. Avoid status-driven or fear-driven messaging.
